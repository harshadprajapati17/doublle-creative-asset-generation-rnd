# AI Image & Video Generation — Market Research

## Objective

Identify the latest AI models, providers, and techniques available for our image and video generation requirements.

## Models / Providers

Research and shortlist:

- Latest image-generation models
- Latest video-generation models
- Available model versions
- API providers
- Open-source vs hosted models

## Image Generation Capabilities

Check:

- Text-to-image
- Image-to-image
- Reference images
- Character/subject consistency
- Image editing
- High-resolution output

## Video Generation Capabilities

Check:

- Text-to-video
- Image-to-video
- Reference image/video support
- Character consistency
- Motion quality
- Camera control
- Video duration
- Output resolution
- Video editing/extension capabilities

## Techniques

Research relevant approaches:

- Prompt engineering
- Reference image workflows
- Image-to-video workflows
- Multi-image/video generation and selection
- Image/video editing
- Model fine-tuning, if required
- Multi-model workflows

## Pricing

Collect:

- API pricing
- Image generation cost
- Video generation cost
- Resolution/quality-based pricing
- Duration-based pricing for video
- Volume pricing, if available

## Output

A shortlist of image and video models, providers, and approaches that should proceed to evaluation.
