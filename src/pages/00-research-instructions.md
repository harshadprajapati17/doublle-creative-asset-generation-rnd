## Research Instruction

Current model knowledge may be outdated.

- Always research and verify the latest information before including a model, version, capability, or pricing.
- Prefer official provider sources for model availability, capabilities, and pricing.
- Clearly mention the date when the information was verified.
- Do not include older, deprecated, or discontinued models unless relevant for comparison.
- If information cannot be verified, mark it as uncertain rather than making assumptions.
